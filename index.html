NYC Buildings & HPD records

DOB assistant

Check any address for open DOB/ECB violations, unsafe-condition notices and open HPD litigation before your buyer gets too far.

On the live site this comes straight from NYC Open Data (DOB, ECB, facade/FISP and HPD vacate records) at no cost. In the Claude preview it uses your Marketproof connection instead, about 4 lookups per building. Results are saved on this device for 24 hours. “Open” means DOB hasn’t marked it resolved, and some open items are old paperwork. Confirm on DOB BIS or with the managing agent.
