1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Pocket Approval LLC ("we," "us") operates Pocket Approval, a building research and deal-tracking tool for real estate and mortgage professionals and the home buyers and sellers they work with, available at pocketapproval.com and in our mobile apps (the "Service").

Our purpose. Pocket Approval is a preliminary building due-diligence and financing feasibility tool. It exists to reduce unnecessary transaction expenses by giving buyers, agents, attorneys and lenders early visibility into building-level financial, operational and financing risks. Put simply: Pocket Approval tells people what they should investigate before they spend money investigating it. It does not replace formal due diligence, legal review or lender underwriting.

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses the Service. It does not apply to third-party websites we link to, such as county record portals.

4. Public records we display

The Service displays building information from government public records, including NYC Open Data and ACRIS, the Miami-Dade Clerk of the Court and Comptroller, the Miami-Dade Property Appraiser, the Florida Department of Revenue, the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation, the New Jersey Treasury and tax list, and HUD.

  • We show lender names from recorded mortgages and summary counts such as financed versus cash sales.
  • We do not display the names of individual buyers, sellers or borrowers. Where a lender on a recorded mortgage is a private individual, we show "Private lender (individual)" instead of a name.
  • Public records can be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Information in the Service is provided for professional research only. It is not an official record, a project approval, or a lending decision, and a negative preliminary assessment is not proof that no mortgage is available.

4A. Private source documents and shared building findings

We treat the documents you upload and the findings derived from them differently.

  • Source documents stay private. Financial statements, budgets, questionnaires, board minutes, insurance certificates, engineering reports and purchase contracts you upload are stored privately in your account. Other users never see them unless you choose to share them.
  • Building-level findings may be shared. Summary findings about the building itself may be added to that building's profile and shown to other users. These are short statements of whether the building meets published agency guidelines, such as "Does not budget 10% for reserves," "No crime/fidelity insurance in place," or "Operating deficit reported," without dollar figures, quotations or page references. Each shows the type and date of its source document and its verification status, for example "based on a FY2025 budget uploaded by a user; not lender verified." The user who uploaded the documents sees the detailed findings privately in their own account.
  • What is never shared: personally identifiable information (including names of unit owners, buyers, sellers, borrowers or board members), unit-level details tied to a person, confidential transaction details such as a purchase price, loan terms or the parties to a deal, your notes, and your identity as the source.
  • Only where permitted. We share findings only where the underlying information was lawfully obtained and its use is permitted, and you can opt out for any upload.
  • Findings are preliminary. A finding classified as a concern or a guideline issue is a reason to investigate, not a conclusion. A negative or incomplete preliminary assessment is not proof that financing is unavailable; portfolio, non-agency and exception lenders may finance buildings that do not meet standard agency requirements.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that host and run the Service for us, under contracts limiting their use of the data: hosting (Vercel), database and file storage (Supabase), AI processing (Anthropic), network services (Fixie), and email delivery. We keep a current list of these providers on our website.
  • People you choose to share with: when you share a building summary, deal summary or calendar entry, or invite teammates.
  • Shared building findings: building-level findings derived from documents you upload, as described in section 4A, may be added to that building's profile and shown to other users, with the source document type, date and verification status. We never share the documents themselves, your notes, your deals, or your identity as the source. You can turn this off for any upload.
  • Legal reasons: to comply with law or valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of users, the public or us.
  • Business transfers: in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Documents you upload

  • Purchase contracts contain other people's names and contact details. Use them only for the transaction they relate to.
  • Documents are stored privately in your account. They are never shown to other users unless you share them.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires. Please keep your login private and tell us at Marc.Paz@marcapazpc.com if you suspect misuse.
